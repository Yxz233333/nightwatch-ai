英文原始摘要

We prove the approximation-paving conjecture of Popa and Vaes for every maximal abelian subalgebra of a complex von Neumann algebra. For each $`0\lt \varepsilon\lt 1`$, every self-adjoint operator is a strong limit of self-adjoint operators of norm at most three times its norm, each admitting a norm paving with error at most ε times the approximant's own norm. The number of projections is at most $`C\varepsilon^{-6}`$ for a universal constant C. No separability or conditional-expectation hypothesis is required.
