英文原始摘要

We prove Donovan's conjecture over every algebraically closed field K of characteristic p > 0. For each fixed K and bound on defect-group order, blocks of finite groups represent only finitely many K-linear Morita equivalence classes. The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes p = 2.
