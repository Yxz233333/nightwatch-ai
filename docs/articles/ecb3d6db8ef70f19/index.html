英文原始摘要

For every n ≥ 4, a complete connected smooth Riemannian n-manifold without boundary satisfying $`-4\Delta+\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits \ge1`$ as a quadratic-form inequality admits a continuous map to a simplicial complex of dimension at most $`n-2`$ whose entire fibers have diameter bounded only in terms of n. The bound is measured in the original metric. This extends the uniform Urysohn width theorem from a pointwise scalar-curvature lower bound to a spectral lower bound.
