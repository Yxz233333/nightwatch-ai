英文原始摘要

We prove the integral scalar-curvature inequality proposed by Gromov. For every dimension n ≥ 3, there is a constant $`a_n\gt 0`$, depending only on n, such that $`\displaystyle \int_M(\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits _g^-)^{n/2}\,dV_g\ge a_n\|M\|`$ for every closed connected oriented smooth n-manifold M and every smooth Riemannian metric g. Here $`\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits _g^-:=\max\{0,-\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits _g\}`$, and $`\|M\|`$ is real simplicial volume. The proof uses the nonnegative-scalar-curvature vanishing theorem of the companion paper on rational inessentiality.
