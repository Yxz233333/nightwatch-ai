Under the finite-level Ramanujan–Arthur decomposition stated in Theorem 1.1, we construct a global Arthur enhancement for every occurring cuspidal excursion parameter at a specified full finite level for a split connected semisimple group over a global function field. A single algebraic SL2 and a commuting Weil centralizer map recover the given parameter by diagonal specialization on the entire Weil group, including inertia. The result does not assert ellipticity, Arthur-packet classification, or a multiplicity formula.
