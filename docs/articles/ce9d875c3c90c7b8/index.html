英文原始摘要

For every prime p ≥ 5, the canonical map $`L_0L_{K(3)}S\to L_0L_{K(2)}L_{K(3)}S`$ for the sphere spectrum S is nonzero on π−3. Consequently, the height-three strong chromatic splitting formula is false in this range, even as an equivalence of underlying $`E(2)`$-local spectra without specified summand maps.
