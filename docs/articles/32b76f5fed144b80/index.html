For the ferromagnetic four-state broadcast model with $`0\lt \lambda\lt 1`$, we prove that reconstruction on a bounded-degree deterministic rooted tree occurs exactly when its L3 capacity with edge resistances $`\lambda^{-2|e|}`$ is positive.
