英文原始摘要

We prove Erdős's conjecture on the largest fibers of Euler's totient function: for every ε > 0, infinitely many positive integers n have more than $`n^{1-\varepsilon }`$ preimages. We also show that, for every fixed δ > 0, there are at least $`x^{1-o(1)}`$ primes p in $`2x\lt p\le5x`$ whose predecessors have no prime factor exceeding xδ.
