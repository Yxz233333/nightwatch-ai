For every fixed treewidth bound, the shortest-path metrics of finite connected graphs with arbitrary positive real edge lengths embed into real L1 with uniformly bounded distortion. This resolves the bounded-treewidth case of the Gupta–Newman–Rabinovich–Sinclair conjecture positively.
