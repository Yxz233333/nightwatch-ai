  • 作者声称证明κ(X) ≥ κ(F) + κ(Z)，即普通Iitaka次可加性。

英文原始摘要

We prove the ordinary Iitaka subadditivity conjecture for surjective projective morphisms with connected fibers between smooth connected projective varieties over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero. If F is the geometric generic fiber of $`f:X\to Z`$, then $`\kappa(X)\geq\kappa(F)+\kappa(Z)`$.
