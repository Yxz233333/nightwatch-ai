We prove the directional zero–one conjecture for nearest-neighbor random walks in independent and identically distributed strictly elliptic environments on ℤd, d ≥ 3: the probability of escape in each fixed nonzero real direction is zero or one. Only strict positivity of the transition probabilities is required; no uniform lower bound or moment assumption is imposed.
