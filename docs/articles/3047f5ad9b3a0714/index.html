英文原始摘要

Replacing the Gaussian rows used to select a finite message by independent rows increases the remaining conditional information by at most $`Cd`$, for a uniform spherical signal, message entropy at most d2, and the specified row dimensions proportional to d. As an application, we prove that learners with $`M=o(d^2)`$ persistent bits need $`T=\Omega(d\log(1/\epsilon))`$ exact observations to attain uniform-sphere angular success at least 3/5, for $`0\lt \epsilon\le1/10`$ and a deterministic finite horizon.
