For every n ≥ 1 and prime $`p\gt n+1`$, we construct a $`2^n`$-stage ordered filtration of $`L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedge`$ with the classical chromatic-splitting cofibers. The map from the first stage to the target is the canonical localization unit.
