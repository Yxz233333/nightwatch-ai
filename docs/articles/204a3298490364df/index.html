英文原始摘要

We prove the equivariant cohomological Hikita conjecture for arbitrary finite quivers, including loops and multiple arrows. For any dimension and framing vectors, any commuting flavor torus, and a stability character whose semistable locus is stable and has free gauge action, the equivariant cohomology of the Nakajima variety is canonically isomorphic to the coordinate ring of the scheme-theoretic fixed locus of the stability cocharacter on the flavor-deformed Coulomb branch. This is an isomorphism of graded algebras over the common coefficient ring, retaining nilpotents and including the empty case.
