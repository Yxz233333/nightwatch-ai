英文原始摘要

We prove that a fixed number of coordinate sweeps of the Thorp shuffle on $`2^d`$ cards makes the squared L2 distance of the full permutation density from uniform tend to zero as $`d\to\infty`$. In particular, the total-variation mixing time is $`\Theta(d)`$ physical shuffles.
