英文原始摘要

We construct, in some sufficiently large fixed finite complex dimension m, a domain in ℂm diffeomorphic to $`\mathbb R^{2m}`$ that admits a complete Kähler metric with real sectional curvature at most −1 and has only constant bounded holomorphic functions. Its sectional curvatures are unbounded below. The construction gives a negative answer to the one-sided bounded-holomorphic-function question.
