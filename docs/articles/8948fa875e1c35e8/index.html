  • 研究对象为正方格点最近邻O(4)模型的转移算子能隙

  • 质量定义涵盖旋转不变局部可观测扇区

For the nearest-neighbor $`O(4)`$ model on the square lattice at inverse temperature β, we prove the exact low-temperature asymptotic $`\displaystyle m_{\mathrm{lat}}(\beta)\sim 32\exp(\pi/4-1/2)\sqrt\beta\,\exp(-\pi\beta) \qquad (\beta\to\infty).`$ Here the mass is the gap of the full Osterwalder–Schrader transfer operator, including rotation-invariant local-observable sectors, in units of one original lattice time step.
