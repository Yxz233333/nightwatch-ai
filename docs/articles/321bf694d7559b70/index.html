英文原始摘要

We prove that the real Banach space ℓ1 has metric Markov cotype two, answering a question of Mendel and Naor. As a consequence, every Lipschitz map from an arbitrary subset of a real Hilbert space into ℓ1 extends to the whole Hilbert space with a universal multiplicative loss in its Lipschitz constant, resolving Ball's extension problem for this target.
