英文原始摘要

We prove that every centered log-concave probability measure with a Lebesgue density on ℝn and linear subgaussian parameter a satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{Ent}}\nolimits _\mu(f^2)\le Ca^2\int|Df|^2\,d\mu`$ for compactly supported smooth f, with one universal constant C. This resolves positively the dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev conjecture for subgaussian log-concave measures.
