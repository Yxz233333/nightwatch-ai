英文原始摘要

We prove that the unique ground state of a uniformly gapped nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian on an $`L\times L`$ square lattice admits a projected entangled-pair state approximation with bond dimension polynomial in L and global vector error at most L−1 after normalization. Only the gap of the full Hamiltonian is assumed. The result is an existence theorem, with constants uniform over Hamiltonians of fixed local dimension, interaction strength, and gap.
