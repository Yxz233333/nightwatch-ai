英文原始摘要

We prove that the rescaled counting measure of a critical square-lattice Fortuin–Kasteleyn Dobrushin interface in the unit square converges to the Minkowski-content measure of its Schramm–Loewner limit, for every fixed cluster weight $`1\le q\lt 4`$. A single deterministic constant times the predicted power of the mesh gives the normalization. Convergence is joint with the ordered curve and includes the total mass, giving the scaling limit of the interface's total number of steps.
