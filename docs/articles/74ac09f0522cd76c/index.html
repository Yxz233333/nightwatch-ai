英文原始摘要

We construct nonzero locally constructible perverse Hecke eigensheaves with Borel level at one marked point on a smooth projective curve of genus at least two over an algebraic closure of a finite field. The parameter is a Zariski-dense geometric ℓ-adic local system with tame regular-unipotent monodromy. The group is simple and simply connected and satisfies four explicit Lie-theoretic characteristic hypotheses. The eigensheaves have parabolic nilpotent singular support, and their eigenisomorphisms are compatible with tensor products, permutations, and fusion.
