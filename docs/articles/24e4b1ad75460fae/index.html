英文原始摘要

We prove integral Donovan finiteness: for every prime p and positive integer M, the blocks of all finite groups with defect groups of order at most M have only finitely many Morita equivalence classes over $`W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)`$. The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes p = 2. The same bounded-defect finiteness holds over each fixed complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with algebraically closed residue field of characteristic p, including ramified rings.
