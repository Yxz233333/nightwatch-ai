英文原始摘要

We prove the asymptotically hyperbolic Penrose inequality for sufficiently small maximal vacuum conformal perturbations of a positive-mass Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter exterior, for every fixed decaying transverse-traceless seed and every solution branch satisfying the stated decay and mass assumptions. The bound uses the area of the marginally outer trapped boundary itself; it requires neither outermostness nor outer area-minimization, and no bulk-versus-boundary domination condition. For sufficiently small positive parameters, equality holds for radial seeds and the inequality is strict otherwise.
