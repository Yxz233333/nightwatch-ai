Pointwise convergence of triple ergodic averages for mixing transformations

  • 声称对任意三个有界可测函数几乎处处收敛到积分之积
  • 结论不依赖定量混合速率假设

英文原始摘要

We prove pointwise convergence of triple ergodic averages for every invertible mixing probability preserving transformation T of an arbitrary probability space $`(X,\mathcal F,\mu)`$, with measurable inverse. For every triple of bounded measurable functions $`f_1,f_2,f_3:X\to\mathbb C`$, $`\displaystyle \frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N f_1(T^nx)f_2(T^{2n}x)f_3(T^{3n}x) \longrightarrow \prod_{j=1}^3\int_X f_j\,d\mu`$ for μ-almost every x as $`N\to\infty`$ through all positive integers.
