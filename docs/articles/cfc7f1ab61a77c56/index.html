英文原始摘要

For the uniform probability measure ρ on a compact convex body in ℝd, d ≥ 2, quadratic optimal transport maps are one-third Hölder continuous in $`L^2(\rho)`$ with respect to the target's 2-Wasserstein distance. The constant is uniform over all targets supported in a fixed compact set, and the exponent is optimal. A three-atom family on a fixed cube disproves Letrouit's conjectured uniform square-root estimate.
