英文原始摘要

For every nonnegative integer n, we construct a Smith–Toda complex $`V(n)`$ at some prime p depending on n. It is a finite p-local spectrum whose Brown–Peterson homology is $`\mathrm{BP}_*/(p,v_1,\ldots,v_n)`$ with its canonical comodule structure and generator in degree zero. Each listed generator is killed to its first power.
