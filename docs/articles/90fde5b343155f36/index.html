英文原始摘要

We prove that, at each singular point of the first singular time of the mean-curvature flow of a smooth compact connected embedded surface without boundary in ℝ3, all fixed-center rescalings converge locally smoothly on compact negative-time intervals to one multiplicity-one homothetic self-shrinker flow. The limit is unique in the original ambient coordinates, including its position and axes. No mean-convexity assumption or prescribed tangent model is required.
