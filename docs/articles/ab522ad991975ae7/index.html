英文原始摘要

We prove a uniform interior $`C^{1,\alpha_d}`$ estimate for bounded infinity-harmonic functions in every dimension d ≥ 3. For some $`\alpha_d\in(0,1/3]`$ depending only on the dimension, the gradient's supremum norm and αd-Hölder seminorm on B1/2 are bounded by a dimension-dependent constant times the oscillation on B1. The exponent is not explicit, and no endpoint or boundary regularity claim is made. In particular, infinity-harmonic functions are locally C1 on every open subset of ℝd, for d ≥ 3.
