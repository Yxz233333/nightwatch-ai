英文原始摘要

We construct nonzero perverse Weil Hecke eigensheaves for SLn with Borel level at one marked point, after a finite extension of the field of constants. The parameter is a geometrically dense arithmetic PGLn-local system with tame regular-unipotent monodromy on a once-punctured curve of genus at least two over a finite field of characteristic p > n. The full multi-leg eigenstructure is Frobenius compatible with the prescribed arithmetic eigenvalue, and the geometric sheaf has parabolic nilpotent singular support.
