英文原始摘要

We prove that, for all integers n ≥ 3 and k ≥ 1 and all positive real capacities $`R_1,\ldots,R_k`$, the closed standard symplectic $`2n`$-balls of these capacities embed disjointly into the interior of a ball of capacity R > 0 if and only if $`\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^k R_i^n\lt R^n, \qquad R_i+R_j\lt R\quad(i\ne j).`$ Capacity is π times the squared Euclidean radius, and each embedding is defined on a neighborhood of its closed source ball. This proves Siegel and Yao's Conjecture A.
