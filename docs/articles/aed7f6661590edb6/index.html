英文原始摘要

We prove the finite case of the Benson–Etingof–Ostrik conjecture: every finite symmetric tensor category over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic p > 0 admits a k-linear exact faithful strong symmetric monoidal functor to a finite higher Verlinde category $`\mathop{\mathrm{Ver}}\nolimits _{p^n}(k)`$. The result includes characteristic two, and the level n may depend on the category.
