英文原始摘要

For SLn in characteristic p > n, we construct nonzero locally constructible perverse Hecke eigensheaves with Borel level at two or more marked points on a smooth projective curve of genus at least two over an algebraic closure of a finite field. The parameter is a Zariski-dense geometric ℓ-adic PGLn-local system with unipotent tame monodromy; its nilpotent logarithms may have any Jordan type, including zero. The eigensheaves have parabolic nilpotent singular support, and their eigenisomorphisms retain the full tensor and fusion structure.
