英文原始摘要

We prove a uniform spectral gap for odd open spin-one antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chains with the same fixed magnetic field at both endpoints. The field $`h=3/5`$ gives a gap greater than $`\log(10)/392`$ on every chain of $`2L+1`$ sites with L ≥ 960. Tasaki's index theorem then gives index −1 for every subsequential local limit of these boundary-selected ground states.
