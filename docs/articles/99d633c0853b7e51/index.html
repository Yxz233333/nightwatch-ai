英文原始摘要

We prove the Selmer converse in coranks zero and one for every elliptic curve over ℚ and every prime p: if the full p-power Selmer group has ℤp-corank $`r\in\{0,1\}`$, then the analytic and Mordell–Weil ranks both equal r, and the entire Tate–Shafarevich group is finite. As an application at the additive prime 3, we prove that for every prime $`\ell\equiv4,7,8\pmod9`$, the cubic $`X^3+Y^3=\ell Z^3`$ has analytic and Mordell–Weil rank one and finite Tate–Shafarevich group. In particular, every such ℓ is a sum of two rational cubes.
