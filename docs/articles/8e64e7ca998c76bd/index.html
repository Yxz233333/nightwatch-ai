英文原始摘要

For every fixed $`0\lt \rho\lt 1`$, the matrix indexed by odd vertex sets U and perfect matchings M of Kn, with entries $`|M\cap\delta(U)|-1+\rho`$, has real positive semidefinite rank $`2^{\Omega(n)}`$ as even n tends to infinity. Here $`\delta(U)`$ is the edge cut of U. Consequently, every exact semidefinite lift of the perfect matching polytope has exponential size.
