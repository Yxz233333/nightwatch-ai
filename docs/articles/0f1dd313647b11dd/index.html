英文原始摘要

We construct two distinct uniformly positive bounded measurable scalar conductivities on a ball in ℝ3 with the same full Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator. Both conductivities equal one near the boundary. This gives nonuniqueness in the scalar Calderón problem at bounded measurable regularity.
