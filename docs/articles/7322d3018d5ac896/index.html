英文原始摘要

For a finite p-group G and a subgroup H, we prove that the optimal chromatic fixed-point loss equals the shortest length of a subnormal chain from H to G with cyclic quotients. The equality holds at every prime and every nonnegative height, resolving positively the equality proposed by Kuhn and Lloyd.
