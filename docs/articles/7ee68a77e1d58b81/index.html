英文原始摘要

For each fixed $`0\lt \kappa\lt 8`$, let $`d=1+\kappa/8`$. The gauge $`h(r)=r^d(\log\log(1/r))^{(2-d)/2}`$ at sufficiently small radii almost surely gives positive finite Hausdorff measure to every nontrivial positive-time compact segment of chordal SLEκ. This gives an explicit solution to Schramm's Hausdorff-measure problem in this parameter range. The entire trace has finite expected measure in every bounded disk. The exponent-one iterated-logarithm gauge suggested by Schramm is not sigma-finite on any such segment.
